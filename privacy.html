Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026.

Flipboom does not collect any personal data.

  • No account, no sign-in, no name, email or location.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, gems, skins, settings) is saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation that a purchase happened, so it can unlock what you bought.

Flipboom is suitable for all ages and works the same for children: nothing is collected from anyone.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: contact us here.

En français

Flipboom ne collecte aucune donnée personnelle : pas de compte, pas d'analytique, pas de pistage, pas de publicité ciblée. Votre progression reste uniquement sur votre iPhone. Les achats intégrés sont traités par Apple, nous ne voyons jamais vos informations de paiement.
