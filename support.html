Flipboom support

How to play

Tap anywhere to flip gravity. The hero walks forward on its own. Avoid the red spikes, break the green cores, grab the star coins and reach the portal. In jump sections a tap jumps, in wave sections hold to fly up and release to dive.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. To get the Premium Pass back on a new iPhone, open SHOP and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
